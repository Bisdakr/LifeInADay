How does this work? · The numbers and the meaning

We compress a reference lifespan into the 930 minutes between 6:30 AM and 10:00 PM. Your exact age in days is divided by the chosen U.S. life expectancy at birth, and that proportion sets the time on the imaginary clock. The sun and color palette follow the same progress. If you've lived longer than that average, the clock shows 10:00 PM+—not an end to your story.

References: U.S. life expectancy at birth, 2024 (final): male 76.5 years, female 81.4 years, all persons 79.0 years. Source: CDC / National Center for Health Statistics, published January 29, 2026. See also United States Life Tables, 2024.

Important: These are period life expectancy averages at birth, not personalized forecasts or deadlines. People who have already reached a given age have different remaining life expectancies; real lifespans vary greatly. The tool does not account for country, personal health, lifestyle, or future changes in mortality. It's art grounded in a statistical reference—not a prediction.
